# SMART BAR User Manual: Bar Owner, Manager, and Sales Accounts

This guide covers everyday workflows for bar owners, managers, and sales users. It does not cover global-owner administration. What you can do depends on your account permissions. If a page or action is not available, contact your bar owner.

## 1. Sign in

1. Sign in with the username and password provided for your account.
2. Use the navigation menu to open a page available to your role.
3. Log out when you finish, especially when using a shared device.

## 2. Sales user: process a sale

1. Open **POS**.
2. Search for a product or choose a category, then select products to add them to the cart.
3. Check quantities and prices. Edit the quantity directly, or use the plus and minus buttons. The line totals and cart total update as you type; entering 0 removes the item. Available stock limits the quantity you can sell.
4. Select a payment method:
   - For a direct sale, choose the method used by the customer.
   - Your manager may add bar-specific methods in **Settings > Business**. Custom methods require a payment reference.
   - For a credit sale, select a registered customer and choose **Credit**. The full sale amount is added to the customer's balance. POS does not currently accept a partial payment at checkout for a credit sale; record payments later through bill management.
5. Review the cart and total, then select **Checkout**.
6. Wait for the success message before completing the handover. If an error appears, correct the details and try again.

Credit is unavailable for walk-in customers. Confirm the selected customer before checking out.

## 3. Sales user and manager: customers

1. Open **Customers** to find and review customer records and credit balances.
2. If **Add Customer** is available, select it and enter the customer's name, phone number, and gender. Add a starting balance only when appropriate, then save.
3. Use the customer information to verify the correct account before processing a credit sale or bill payment. Custom payment methods configured by the bar manager are available for bill payments and require a payment reference.

## 4. Sales user and manager: orders

1. Open **Orders-Sales** to review recorded orders.
2. Find the order and inspect its items, amount, payment details, and status.
3. If the **Reverse** action is available and a sale must be cancelled, verify the order before confirming. Reversing restores stock and adjusts the customer's balance.

## 5. Sales user and manager: payment history

1. Open **Payment History**.
2. Choose a period or set custom start and end dates. Filter by payment type or customer if needed.
3. Review the payment records and summaries. Use **Export Excel** to download the displayed history.
4. If you have permission to manage settlements, review pending bill-payment records and confirm or reject them only after checking the payment details. A submitted payment request is not confirmed until it has been approved.

## 6. Manager: cash chest

Managers with cash-chest permissions can reconcile the bar's physical cash:

1. Open **Cash Chest**. If no session is open, enter the opening float and an optional note, then select **Open Cash Chest**.
2. Record cash received or removed using **Record cash movement**. Enter the amount and a clear reason.
3. At closing, count the physical cash, enter the counted amount, and add a note for any difference. Select **Close Cash Session**.
4. Compare expected cash, counted cash, and variance. Review recent closed sessions when needed.

Sales users can view the current cash-chest information when available, but opening, recording movements, and closing are manager/owner actions.

## 7. Dashboard

Open **Dashboard** to review the sales and collection summaries available to your account. Sales, payments collected, and outstanding credit are different figures: a credit sale contributes to sales, while its unpaid amount remains outstanding. The **Expected Handover** figure may include credit payments collected separately from the original sale.

## 8. Bar owner: manage the bar

### Set up categories and products

1. Open **Categories** and select **Add Category**. Enter a name and optional description, then save.
2. Open **SMART BAR** and select **Add Product** or **Batch Add**.
3. Enter the product name, category, cost price, selling price, current stock, unit, and optional low-stock threshold.
4. Save the product. Edit it when its details or prices change. Keep stock accurate so POS availability is reliable.

### Manage suppliers and purchase orders

1. Open **Suppliers** and select **Add Supplier**. Enter the supplier name and phone; contact person, email, address, and notes are optional.
2. Open **Purchase Orders** and select **New Purchase Order**.
3. Choose a supplier if applicable. Search for products, select each one, and enter the quantity and unit cost. Add other items as needed.
4. Add an expected delivery date or notes, then create the order.
5. Update the order status as the purchase progresses. Mark it **Received** only after the goods arrive.

### Record inventory adjustments

1. Open **Inventory** and create a new adjustment.
2. Select the product and adjustment type: wastage, damage, return to supplier, restock, or count correction.
3. Enter the quantity and a clear reason, then save.
4. Review the adjustment history if you need to check a previous stock change.

### Manage staff and settings

1. Open **Settings** and use **Profile** to update your account information.
2. Use **Team** to create sales accounts. Provide the staff member's name, username, email, and password, then share credentials securely.
3. Use the team list to reset a sales account password or remove an account when necessary.
4. Use **Password** to change your own password. Use **Business** to update the bar's details, currency, and payment methods. Cash, Airtel Money, Mpamba, and Bank Account are preloaded but can be removed; custom methods apply to this bar only and appear in checkout, bill payments, payment history, and reports. The chosen currency label applies to price displays and receipts for this bar only; other bars are unchanged. Select a listed currency or choose **Other (custom currency)** and enter the currency code or symbol. Changing the label does not convert saved amounts or apply exchange rates.

### Apply for a bar

If you are registering a new bar through the public **Apply for a Bar** form, enter the bar and owner details, including the bar's country and district. These location details are saved with the application and bar.

### Reports and audit history

- Open **Reports & Analytics** to select Today, Week, Month, Year, or a custom date range. For a custom report, enter the start and end dates; refresh to reload the results.
- Open **Audit Log** to review recorded activity when you need to trace changes.
- Use **Payment History** to review collections by date, type, or customer, and export the displayed history when required.

Bar owners can also use **Orders-Sales**, **Customers**, **Cash Chest**, and **Dashboard** as described above. For cash reconciliation, the owner can open a cash session, record cash movements, and close it with a physical cash count.

## 9. Good operating habits

- Check the product, quantity, customer, payment method, and amount before checkout.
- Confirm that a customer payment is approved before treating it as collected.
- Record cash movements with accurate amounts and clear reasons.
- Count cash before closing a session and explain any variance.
- If a page is unavailable, a transaction fails, or a balance looks incorrect, note the bar, customer/order, amount, and time, then contact your bar owner.
